# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

## Overview

OctoAcme uses a comprehensive, phase-based project management methodology designed to ensure consistent, repeatable project execution. Our processes emphasize clear communication, risk management, and continuous improvement throughout the project lifecycle.

### Project Management Approach

The OctoAcme methodology is built on the following core principles:

- **Centralized Knowledge**: All project management processes are documented in this Copilot Space to provide equal access to processes, decisions, and rationale across the team
- **Phase-Based Execution**: Projects move through distinct phases—Initiation, Planning, Execution & Tracking, Release & Deployment, and Retrospective & Continuous Improvement
- **Risk-Aware Planning**: Proactive identification and management of risks, with clear escalation paths and stakeholder communication protocols
- **Continuous Improvement**: Regular retrospectives and process refinement to enhance project execution and team performance
- **Clear Accountability**: Well-defined roles and personas ensure every team member understands their responsibilities and contributions

This centralized approach serves multiple purposes:
- **Onboarding Tool**: New team members can quickly understand OctoAcme's project management processes and find relevant documentation
- **Navigation Hub**: Eliminates scattered knowledge and provides searchable, versioned artifacts in one location
- **Consistency**: Ensures all team members have equal access to processes, decisions, and rationale
- **Accessibility**: Makes tacit team insights and institutional knowledge easily discoverable
- **Single Source of Truth**: Reduces dependency on individual team members for process knowledge

## Getting Started

### For New Team Members
1. Start with [Project Management Overview](./octoacme-project-management-overview.md) to understand OctoAcme's overall approach
2. Review [Roles and Personas](./octoacme-roles-and-personas.md) to understand team structure and your responsibilities
3. Familiarize yourself with the full [Project Lifecycle Phases](#project-lifecycle-phases) to understand how projects flow through OctoAcme

### For Project Leads
1. Begin with [Project Initiation](./octoacme-project-initiation.md) when starting a new project
2. Reference [Project Planning](./octoacme-project-planning.md) for detailed planning guidance
3. Consult [Risks and Communication](./octoacme-risks-and-communication.md) throughout the project for risk and stakeholder management
4. Use [Execution and Tracking](./octoacme-execution-and-tracking.md) for day-to-day execution guidance
5. Follow [Release and Deployment](./octoacme-release-and-deployment.md) as the project approaches delivery
6. Conduct learning activities using [Retrospective and Continuous Improvement](./octoacme-retrospective-and-continuous-improvement.md)

### For Individual Contributors
1. Review [Roles and Personas](./octoacme-roles-and-personas.md) to understand your role and responsibilities
2. Check [Execution and Tracking](./octoacme-execution-and-tracking.md) for guidance on executing your assigned work
3. Reference other documents as needed based on your project's phase
